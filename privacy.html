Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

gws-cli is a personal tool used only by its owner to access their own Google account through the Google Workspace APIs.

  • Data accessed through Google APIs is used only on the owner's own computer, at the owner's direction.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • OAuth credentials are stored encrypted on the owner's device and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
